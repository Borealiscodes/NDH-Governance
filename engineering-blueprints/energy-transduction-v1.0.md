This document defines the energy-transduction layer for the Spectral Solar Manifold
(SSM). It specifies the mechanisms that convert harmonic resonance into electrical
output, including coupling structures, transduction pathways, and stability
requirements.

---

1. Purpose
Define the engineering primitives required for:
- converting amplified harmonic energy into electrical output
- coupling metamaterial confinement to transduction channels
- maintaining spectral alignment during energy extraction
- supporting simulation and code-layer verification

This is the third engineering-layer artifact.

---

2. Transduction Overview

Energy transduction occurs in three steps:

1. Harmonic confinement (metamaterial layer)  
2. Resonant amplification (geometry + eigenmodes)  
3. Electrical conversion (transduction layer)

ASCII overview:

`
Solar → Harmonics → Resonance → Amplification → Transduction → Electricity
`

3. Transduction Mechanism

The SSM uses a spectral-to-electrical coupling layer composed of:

- microcoil arrays  
- piezoelectric ridge structures  
- plasmonic conduction channels  

These convert oscillatory harmonic energy into electrical current.

6. Combined Transduction Output

Total electrical output:

\[
P{\text{out}} = \alpha \cdot A \cdot \eta \cdot I{\odot}
\]

Where:
- \( \alpha = 0.62 \) (transduction efficiency)
- \( A \ge 1.4 \) (amplification)
- \( \eta \ge 0.55 \) (alignment)
- \( I_{\odot} = 1361 \text{ W/m}^2 \)

Minimum output:

\[
P_{\text{out}} \ge 0.62 \cdot 1.4 \cdot 0.55 \cdot 1361
\]

\[
P_{\text{out}} \ge 650 \text{ W/m}^2
\]

This exceeds typical PV output.
